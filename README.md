## Why not the 7-year rule?

The multiply-by-7 formula has no scientific basis. Dogs age rapidly in their first two years and then slow down. Size also matters significantly — a small dog of 10 years has 56 human years while a large dog of the same age has 66.

Each species on Animal Calculator Online uses its own non-linear aging curve based on veterinary and longevity research.
